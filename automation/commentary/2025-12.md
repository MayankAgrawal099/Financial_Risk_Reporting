# December 2025 management comment
FY2025 net income was $1.96M against a budget of $3.58M (-45.2%). Revenue was close to plan (-1.5%), so the gap comes from credit losses: provisions were $4.82M against $3.18M budgeted, almost entirely because the loss rate was above budget, not because the loan book grew faster. Operating expenses were 4.5% over budget. Lower income tax (+$0.54M) cushions the result but does not offset it. The scorecard under-predicted the 2015 default rate (14.8% actual vs 13.0% predicted). Proposed actions: reset the provision rate assumption in the next budget and recalibrate the scorecard before it is used for provisioning.
