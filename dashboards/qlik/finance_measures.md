# Finance sheet: master measures

Generated by `src/export_for_qlik.py`. Create each one in Qlik Sense under Master items > Measures: paste the expression, set the number format with the format pattern, and use the name as shown.

| Name | Expression | Format pattern | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total Revenue | `Sum({<[P&L Line]={'Total Revenue'}>} Actual)` | `#,##0` | Actual revenue before funding costs and credit losses (M03). |
| Total Revenue Budget | `Sum({<[P&L Line]={'Total Revenue'}>} Budget)` | `#,##0` | Budgeted revenue (M03). |
| Net Interest Income | `Sum({<[P&L Line]={'Net Interest Income'}>} Actual)` | `#,##0` | Interest earned minus interest paid (M05). |
| Provision for Credit Losses | `Sum({<[P&L Line]={'Provision for Credit Losses'}>} Actual)` | `#,##0` | Actual provision expense (M07). |
| Operating Expenses | `Sum({<[P&L Line]={'Operating Expenses'}>} Actual)` | `#,##0` | Actual running costs (M08). |
| Net Income | `Sum({<[P&L Line]={'Net Income'}>} Actual)` | `#,##0` | Actual profit after tax (M11). |
| Net Income Budget | `Sum({<[P&L Line]={'Net Income'}>} Budget)` | `#,##0` | Budgeted profit after tax (M11). |
| Net Income Variance | `Sum({<[P&L Line]={'Net Income'}>} Variance)` | `+#,##0;-#,##0` | Net income minus budget. Positive = favourable (M30). |
| Net Income Variance % | `Sum({<[P&L Line]={'Net Income'}>} Variance) / Sum({<[P&L Line]={'Net Income'}>} Budget)` | `+0.0%;-0.0%` | Net income variance as a share of budget (M31). |
| Net Profit Margin | `Sum({<[P&L Line]={'Net Income'}>} Actual) / Sum({<[P&L Line]={'Total Revenue'}>} Actual)` | `0.0%` | Net income divided by total revenue (M12). |
| Cost-to-Income Ratio | `Sum({<[P&L Line]={'Operating Expenses'}>} Actual) / Sum({<[P&L Line]={'Net Revenue'}>} Actual)` | `0.0%` | Operating expenses divided by net revenue. Lower is better (M13). |
| Actual | `Sum(Actual)` | `#,##0` | Actual for the P&L lines in the table (use with the P&L Line dimension). |
| Budget | `Sum(Budget)` | `#,##0` | Budget for the P&L lines in the table. |
| Variance | `Sum(Variance)` | `+#,##0;-#,##0` | Favourable variance is positive for revenue and cost lines alike (M30). |
| Variance % | `Sum(Variance) / Sum(Budget)` | `+0.0%;-0.0%` | Variance divided by budget (M31). |
| Opex Variance by Cost Centre | `Sum({<[Account ID]={">=6000<=6500"}>} [Account Variance])` | `+#,##0;-#,##0` | Operating expense variance (accounts 6000-6500); use with the Cost Center dimension. |
| Volume Effect | `Sum([Volume Effect])` | `+#,##0;-#,##0` | Part of the variance caused by volume differing from budget (M32). |
| Rate Effect | `Sum([Rate Effect])` | `+#,##0;-#,##0` | Part of the variance caused by the rate differing from budget (M33). |
| Other Effect | `Sum([Other Effect])` | `+#,##0;-#,##0` | Variance on lines with no volume driver. |
